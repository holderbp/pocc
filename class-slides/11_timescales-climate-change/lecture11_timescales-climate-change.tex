\begin{frame}{Forcing: Adding an atmosphere instantaneously}


\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{images/dessler_fig6-1}
\end{center}

{\small
In this figure from Dessler, we see how an \underline{equilibrium} Earth with no atmosphere (with an equilibrium temperature of \unit{255}{\kelvin} due to $F_\text{earth} = F_\text{sun} \; \rightarrow \; \sigma T^4 = \frac{S}{4}(1-\alpha)$) abruptly changes to an \underline{out-of-equilibrium} Earth with an atmosphere.
The imbalance in the middle diagram exhibits a \unit{119}{\watt/\meter^2} {\bf forcing} on the climate system.  Equilibrium is restored in the final picture (our prior calculation of a single-atmosphere $30\degree$C surface temperature).
}


\end{frame}
